## Requirements
### Requirement: Direct Video URL Playback

The system SHALL support synchronized playback of video files from public URLs in addition to YouTube videos.

#### Scenario: Load direct video URL

- **WHEN** a user enters a public video URL (e.g., `https://example.com/video.mp4`)
- **THEN** the system SHALL detect it is not a YouTube URL
- **AND** the system SHALL load the video in an HTML5 video player
- **AND** all participants SHALL see the same video

#### Scenario: Supported video formats

- **WHEN** the video URL points to a supported format (MP4, WebM, OGG, or HLS/M3U8)
- **THEN** the system SHALL attempt playback using the browser's native capabilities

#### Scenario: CORS-blocked video

- **WHEN** the video URL is blocked by CORS policy
- **THEN** the system SHALL display a clear error message explaining the video cannot be loaded
- **AND** the error message SHALL suggest the user try a different source

#### Scenario: Invalid or inaccessible video

- **WHEN** the video URL returns an error (404, 500, network failure)
- **THEN** the system SHALL display a user-friendly error message
- **AND** the previous video state SHALL be preserved

### Requirement: Video Type Detection

The system SHALL automatically detect whether user input is a YouTube video or a direct URL.

#### Scenario: YouTube URL detection

- **WHEN** a user enters a YouTube URL or 11-character video ID
- **THEN** the system SHALL use the YouTube player

#### Scenario: Direct URL detection

- **WHEN** a user enters a URL starting with `http://` or `https://` that does not match YouTube patterns
- **THEN** the system SHALL use the direct video player

### Requirement: Direct Video Synchronization

All playback controls for direct videos SHALL be synchronized across participants.

#### Scenario: Play/pause sync for direct video

- **WHEN** any participant plays or pauses a direct video
- **THEN** all other participants SHALL have their video played or paused accordingly

#### Scenario: Seek sync for direct video

- **WHEN** any participant seeks to a new position in a direct video
- **THEN** all other participants SHALL seek to the same position within ~1 second tolerance

#### Scenario: Playback rate sync for direct video

- **WHEN** any participant changes the playback rate
- **THEN** all other participants SHALL have their playback rate updated

#### Scenario: Volume/mute sync for direct video

- **WHEN** any participant changes volume or mute state
- **THEN** all other participants SHALL have their volume/mute state updated

### Requirement: Video Source Switching

Users SHALL be able to switch between YouTube and direct video sources.

#### Scenario: Switch from YouTube to direct video

- **WHEN** a direct video URL is loaded while a YouTube video is playing
- **THEN** the YouTube player SHALL be replaced with the direct video player
- **AND** the new video SHALL start loading for all participants

#### Scenario: Switch from direct video to YouTube

- **WHEN** a YouTube URL is loaded while a direct video is playing
- **THEN** the direct video player SHALL be replaced with the YouTube player
- **AND** the new video SHALL start loading for all participants
